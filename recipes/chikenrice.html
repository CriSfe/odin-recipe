Chicken rice | Recipe

This is the final dish presentation on Adrina's channel

This dish is representative all over Colombia, there's different variations depending on the region, but mostly implies rice, chicken and vegetables.

Check out the video Chicken rice of Mantequiando con Adriana

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast
  • Purple onion
  • White onion
  • Garlic
  • Beans
  • Carrots
  • Pimenton or paprika

Steps

  1. Boil the chicken breast whit the purple onion and garlic.
  2. Cut the beans and the carrots into pieces, then boil them.
  3. Cut the pimentón (paprika) and white onion into long strips, then fry with the garlic until cooked.
  4. Using two forks try to separate the already cooked chicken breast.

  5. Making the rice

    • Add the rice to the pan to speed up the cooking process.
    • Then add the water from boiling the chicken breasts and cook it.
  6. Fry the beans and carrots, then fry the chicken breasts with the paprika and white onion.
  7. Finally mix well these preparations.

    8. EXTRA: You can fry the potatoes and peel them into sticks for a beautiful presentation.
