Colombian spaghetti chicken | Recipe

This is the final dish presentation on Adrina's channel

This dish is representative all over Colombia, there's different variations depending on the region, but mostly implies rice, chicken and vegetables.

Check out the video Colombian spaghetti chicken of Mantequiando con Adriana

Ingredients

  • Half an white onion
  • 4 Tomatoes
  • 3 Garlics
  • 1 Chicken breasts
  • Spaguetti

Steps

  1. Boil the chicken breasts with garlic, half an onion and spices.
  2. Once the chicken is cooked, add the tomatoes and boil them until the skins can be peeled off.
  3. Peel and crush the tomatoes, then put them on to boil, adding some spices. Once this is done, add some chicken broth.
  4. Boil the spaguetti until it's dente.
  5. Add the chicken breasts preparation with the spaguetti and mix well.
