Archucha with minced beef | Recipe

This is the final dish presentation on Adrina's channel

This dish is representative all over Colombia, there's different variations depending on the region, but mostly implies rice, chicken and vegetables.

Check out the video Archucha with meat of Mantequiando con Adriana

Ingredients

  • Ahogado (Tradicional sauce)

    • Long onion
    • Garlic
    • Fresh tomato
    • Salt
    • Tomato paste

  • Minced beef

  • Bechamel sauce

    • Butter
    • Plain flour
    • Milk

  • Archucha or Colombian pickle

  • Mozzarella cheese

Steps

  1. Make the traditional sauce (Ahogado) Cut the onion into small pieces. Also cut the garlic and crush the tomato. Add salt and tomato paste to a frying pan over a medium heat.
  2. Boil the Colombian pickles and remove their seeds.
  3. Make the béchamel sauce by melting the butter, adding the plain flour, and gradually pouring in the milk.
  4. Fry the minced beef until is cooked, then add the traditional sauce "Ahogado".
  5. Make a béchamel sauce and pour it into the pan. Arrange the Colombian pickles in a single layer and fill the spaces with the cooked minced beef. Add mozzarella cheese on top.
  6. Finally boil the preparation for little while, and then you're ready to serve this dish!.
